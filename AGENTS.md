# Project working agreement
Read ANURAG_WRITING_GUIDE.md here or in the My Projects parent when available. Use Anurag's supplied voice defaults otherwise. Do not invent personal experiences or imitate a named novelist.

After every substantial milestone: validate, commit focused changes, push to the configured remote, and verify remote HEAD matches local HEAD. A local commit is not a remote backup. Never report a successful push without confirmation. Do not force-push. Keep credentials, browser sessions, dependencies, and generated learner data out of Git.

Keep pedagogical simulations separate from published empirical claims. Do not tune data to force a preferred winner. State assumptions, uncertainty, and where a simplified auction differs from production. Keep planned chapters explicitly marked until complete.
